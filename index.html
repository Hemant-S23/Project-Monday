Trading Concepts: "R-Multiple" & Live Equity

1. "+4.20R" kya hota hai? (What is R?)

Trading aur Quantitative Risk Management me "R" ka matlab hota hai R-Multiple (Risk Unit).

1R = 1 Trade par maximum predefined risk (1% of $2,000 = $20.00)
  • Agar kisi trade par aapka $20 ka Stop Loss hai aur aap +$50 profit banate ho, toh wo +2.5R hota hai ($50 ÷ $20).
  • Agar trade Stop Loss hit karti hai, toh exactly -1.0R (-$20) loss hota hai.
  • +4.20R ka matlab hai ki past closed trades me aapne apne 1R risk ka 4.2× net profit (+$84.12) banaya hai!

2. Trade lene par Balance badha/ghata kyu nahi?

💵 Cash Balance (Settled)

Yeh amount tabhi settle hota hai jab trade Close hoti hai (Take Profit, Stop Loss ya manual close button dabane par).

📈 Net Equity (Live Floating)

Jab trade Open hoti hai, toh price ke har tick ke sath aapka Live PnL aur Net Equity realtime me up/down move karta hai!

💡 Ab MONDAY terminal ke header me Active Live Floating PnL aur Net Equity har Binance live tick ke sath realtime me move karega!
